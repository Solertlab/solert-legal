Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Solert ("Solert", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile application that surfaces relevant opportunities across the Solana ecosystem. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. We have designed Solert to collect as little as possible and to keep you in control of your wallet at all times.

The short version. Solert is non-custodial. We never ask for, receive, or store your private keys or seed phrase. When you connect a wallet, Solert reads your public wallet address and on-chain holdings on a read-only basis to personalize what you see. We do not move, spend, or transact your funds.

1. Information we handle

a. Wallet information (public, read-only)

When you connect a wallet using the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter, Solert receives your public wallet address and can read your on-chain token holdings directly from the Solana network. This is used solely to match opportunities to the assets you already hold. This read is read-only:

  • We never receive your private keys, seed phrase, or recovery phrase.
  • We never initiate, sign, or request transactions on your behalf.
  • Connecting is approved in your own wallet app; you can disconnect at any time.

b. Your preferences

To personalize your feed, Solert stores the categories and projects you choose to follow (for example, "Airdrops", "DeFi", or specific protocols). You can change or clear these at any time in the app.

c. Notifications

If you enable push notifications, Solert uses a device push token (provided by your operating system's notification service) so we can alert you about time-sensitive opportunities. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or your device settings.

d. Basic app and diagnostic data

We may collect limited, non-identifying technical information — such as app version, device type, general usage events, and crash/error logs — to keep the app working and improve it. This data is not used to identify you personally.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or government identifiers to use the app's core features.
  • We do not collect or store private keys, seed phrases, or passwords.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. How we use information

  • To personalize the opportunities shown to you, based on your holdings and your chosen preferences.
  • To deliver notifications you have opted into.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the app.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Infrastructure and service providers that help us run the app (for example, hosting, push-notification delivery, and crash reporting), who may process data only on our behalf.
  • The Solana network, which is a public blockchain. Note that your wallet address and its activity are already public on-chain, independently of Solert.
  • Authorities, where required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Data retention

We keep information only for as long as needed to provide the app and for legitimate operational or legal purposes. Preferences and connection data are removed when you disconnect your wallet or clear your data in the app.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the limited information Solert handles. Because Solert is non-custodial, the security of your funds remains governed by your own wallet. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect your wallet at any time to stop on-chain reads.
  • Edit or clear your followed categories and projects in the app.
  • Turn off notifications in the app or your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete information we hold. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

8. Children

Solert is not directed to children under the age required by your jurisdiction (generally under 18), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. International users

Solert may be used globally. By using the app, you understand that limited information may be processed in countries other than your own, consistent with this policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide notice in the app.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at: solertapp@gmail.com.
